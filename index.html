Chicago Essentials

Chicago has a food scene that ranges from famous classics to modern restaurants and neighborhood favorites. For a first visit, trying a few iconic Chicago foods is an easy way to experience the city and learn more about its culture.

Where to Go for Dinner

Chicago has options for almost every kind of dinner, from casual pizza spots to trendy restaurants. Areas like the West Loop, River North, Chinatown, and Wicker Park are great places to start.
